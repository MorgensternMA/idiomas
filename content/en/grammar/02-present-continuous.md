## Cuándo se usa

- **Ahora mismo:** *I'm cooking, call me later.*
- **Algo temporal, "estos días":** *I'm reading a book about Japan.* (no necesariamente en este segundo)
- **Planes ya arreglados, con fecha:** *I'm meeting Ana on Saturday.* (ver *Futuro*)

## Cómo se forma

**am / is / are + verbo-ing**

| | Afirmativa | Negativa | Pregunta |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | I**'m** work**ing** | I**'m not** working | **Am** I working? |
| he / she / it | she**'s** working | she **isn't** working | **Is** she working? |
| you / we / they | they**'re** working | they **aren't** working | **Are** they working? |

Ortografía del *-ing*:

- *e* final se cae: *make → making*.
- Consonante-vocal-consonante corta duplica: *run → running, sit → sitting*.
- *-ie → -ying*: *lie → lying*.

## Verbos que casi nunca van en continuo

Los que describen estados y no acciones: *know, like, love, want, need, understand, believe, seem, own*.

- *I'm knowing the answer.* → *I **know** the answer.*
- *I'm wanting a coffee.* → *I **want** a coffee.*

## Presente simple vs. continuo

| Present simple | Present continuous |
|---|---|
| *I work in an office.* (siempre) | *I'm working from home this week.* (por ahora) |
| *She drives to work.* | *She's driving, she can't answer.* |
